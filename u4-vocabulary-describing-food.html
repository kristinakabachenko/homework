The description recipe
  1. Kind: It's a kind of fruit / vegetable / seafood…
  2. Look: It's green / long and thin / the size of my hand.
  3. Parts: It has a thick skin / a stone / lots of seeds.
  4. Taste: It's sweet / bitter / mild. It's soft / hard.
  5. Eat: You usually eat it raw / grilled / roast / in a salad.

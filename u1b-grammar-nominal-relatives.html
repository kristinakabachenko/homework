📌 Cheat sheet: the rules from our lesson
what = the thing(s) thatNo noun before it. ✗ everything what · the video what
how = the wayOne or the other. ✗ the way how
Emphasis (cleft)What I need is a break. · All I want is… · What she did was (to)…
That's just / exactly / precisely + what…That's exactly what I meant!
whoever / whatever / whenever / wherever / however= any person who / anything that / any time that / any place that / any way that
whichever= the one(s) that, from a limited choice. Whichever of you…
who vs whoeverwhoever = any person. ✗ Who broke it should pay.
+ to-infinitivewhat / how / where / who / when to… ✗ why to

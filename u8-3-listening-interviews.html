STRATEGY · Identifying different types of linking words
When someone talks, the events and ideas are connected by different types of linking words which serve different purposes, for example, to show …
  • contrast: although, even though, though, yet, despite, in spite of, whereas, however
  • reason and result: since, because, because of, as, so
  • sequencing: after, before, then, later, next
  • examples: for instance, for example, such as, like

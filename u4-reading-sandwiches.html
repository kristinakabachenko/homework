Pete's Eats Blog

I travel a lot, and I'm always looking for a new recipe to try at home. But I've just realised that I've never written about the most popular lunch in the world: the humble sandwich! So here are my four favourite sandwich places.

AL'As du Fallafel, Paris

Want a meal that is cheap but tasty? Go here first. Get ready to wait in a long queue at lunchtime, because everyone in Paris seems to know this place. The falafel sandwich is the best I've ever had: crispy chickpea balls with grilled aubergine, cabbage, hummus, tahini and hot sauce. You can take it away, or try to find a seat in the tiny room at the back.

BBanhmibistro, Ho Chi Minh City

A banh mi (say "bun mee") is a Vietnamese sandwich in a French baguette, and Banhmibistro does some of the best. They bake all the bread on the premises every morning. Then you choose your filling from a variety of local Vietnamese ingredients: coriander, fish sauce, pickled carrots, radishes and hot peppers. My favourite is the Bistro Special, with barbecue pork, ham and pâté.

CO'Briens Sandwich Bar, Dublin

These people really know how to make a sandwich! They cut thick slices of fresh bread and fill them with lots of tasty things. Try the turkey and brie with cranberry: it's excellent. Good news for students: there's a student discount, and the normal prices are reasonable too. The company is growing fast and now has sandwich bars in several countries. I just hope the quality stays the same!

DThe Black Camel, Vienna

This famous family restaurant has been open since 1618! The food in the restaurant is expensive, but the trendy deli next to it sells freshly made sandwiches at fair prices. A popular filling is herring salad; others are blood sausage, mushroom salad, and ham with lentils. Tourists can pay a little more for a packed lunch in a gift box with the restaurant's logo on it: a black camel in a headdress.
